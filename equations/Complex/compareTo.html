There is no natural linear ordering for complex numbers. In fact, any square in an ordered field is >= 0 but in the complex field we have that i^2 = -1.

A possible comparison strategy involves comparing the modulus/magnitude abs of the two complex numbers.

In this implementation, we compare two Complex instances by looking at their modulus/magnitude.

This ordering is not consistent with ==: two different numbers with the same modulus (such as 1 and i) compare as equal. A number with a NaN part is greater than any other number, as in double.compareTo.

Implementation

@override
int compareTo(Complex other) => abs().compareTo(other.abs());
